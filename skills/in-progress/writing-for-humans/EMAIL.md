# Email

The delta for email. The voice and the tests live in [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md). Email is external register unless the recipient is a colleague you would DM.

- Subject is the topic in two to five words: "Settlement and reconciliation", "Sandbox IP whitelisting", "OpenAPI specification".
- Opening: "Hello <Name>," or "Hello all," (or "Hello," for a group), a blank line, then a first sentence that carries the point or the frame: "as we discussed, let me write down my understanding of the settlement process." or "I had 2 questions, but we were already over the time:".
- Bullets for parallel questions or findings, each self-contained so the reader can answer it by quoting it back.
- The close is one of: the question you need answered, "Thank you.", or "Please let me know if I got something wrong." No sign-off block; the mail client adds the signature.
- A reminder is one line: "Hello, just reminding about this after the weekend so that it isn't forgotten."
- Replying to several points: quote each point you answer and put the answer under it, so the thread stays readable in the other person's client.
- A long explanatory email is fine when the reader asked for the reasoning. Even then, name the unknowns at the end ("there are a lot of unknowns, but for many of them the vendor can provide guidance") instead of papering over them.
